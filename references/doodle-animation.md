# Doodle Animation

Reference images are optional. Preserve uneven, deliberately amateur pen or pencil lines and a fixed paper background.

## 13 - Stick Figure Comes Alive

Create a seamless looping GIF of a ridiculously simple, poorly drawn stick figure on white notebook paper. The stick figure suddenly comes alive, blinks, looks around curiously, and waves enthusiastically at the viewer. Its movements become increasingly goofy before smoothly returning to its original pose. Preserve the crude, wobbly black pen doodle style and paper texture. Make the animation funny, fluid, and seamlessly looping.

## 14 - Dancing Doodle Monster

Create a seamless looping GIF of a silly, badly drawn little monster with uneven eyes, tiny horns, and crooked legs, scribbled on notebook paper. The monster comes alive and starts dancing, gradually exaggerating its hilarious body wiggles, bouncing feet, and ridiculous expressions before smoothly returning to its starting dance pose. Keep the rough hand-drawn ink style, fixed paper background, and consistent appearance. Make it funny and seamlessly looping.

## 15 - Adorable Doodle Cat

Create a seamless looping GIF of an adorable but hilariously poorly drawn cartoon cat on white paper, made of simple uneven black pen strokes. The doodle cat comes alive, blinks its oversized eyes, wiggles its ears, and enthusiastically waves a tiny paw while its tail swishes back and forth. Then smoothly returns to its original cute pose. Preserve the charming, amateurish sketch style. Make the animation expressive, fluid, and seamlessly looping.

## 16 - Scared by Its Own Shadow

Create a funny seamless looping GIF of a clumsily hand-drawn stick figure on notebook paper. The little doodle suddenly notices its own shadow, gets hilariously startled, jumps into the air with wildly exaggerated cartoon eyes and trembling limbs, then sheepishly returns to its original position. Keep the drawing extremely simple, with wobbly pencil lines and a static paper background. Make the animation comical, fluid, and seamlessly looping.

## 17 - Scribbled Dinosaur Sneeze

Create a hilarious seamless looping GIF of a ridiculously crude, childlike drawing of a tiny dinosaur on white notebook paper. The doodle dinosaur comes alive, scrunches its face, takes an exaggerated breath, and lets out an enormous cartoon sneeze that makes its little body wobble. Then it smoothly recovers to its original pose. Preserve the messy hand-drawn lines, simple shapes, and fixed paper background. Make it funny, expressive, and seamlessly looping.

## 18 - Escape from the Drawn Box

Create a seamless looping GIF of a tiny, badly drawn stick figure trapped inside a hand-drawn square on white notebook paper. The stick figure comes alive, looks around suspiciously, tries to climb over the square's border, hilariously slips back inside, and returns to its original frustrated pose, ready to try again. Use charmingly crude black pen doodle art with playful cartoon physics. Keep the paper and framing consistent. Make the animation fluid, funny, and seamlessly looping.
